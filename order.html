How To Place An Order

From selecting product SKUs and specifying custom branding to volume pricing approval and white-glove global delivery.

01 Step 01

Select Products & Quantity

Choose from our 5 primary corporate collections or request a 1-of-1 bespoke hamper. Minimum order quantities (MOQs) start from 5 to 25 units per SKU.

  • Browse Hampers, Tech, Leather & Drinkware
  • Mix & match items for orders over $7,500
  • Sample box evaluation available on request
MOQ Starting: 5–25 Units
02 Step 02

Specify Customization

Select your preferred branding methods — Laser Monogramming, 24k Gold Hot-Foil Stamping, Blind Crest Embossing, or Pantone Custom Color Matching.

  • Upload vector logo files (SVG, AI, EPS, PDF)
  • Individual recipient monogramming available
  • Bespoke silk-lined wooden trunks & sleeves
Branding Proof: 24 Hours
03 Step 03

Quote & 3D Proof Approval

Receive an instant volume-tiered price quote with bulk discounts up to 30% off, along with high-resolution 3D digital artwork proofs for final sign-off.

  • Automatic volume tier discounts (15% to 30% off)
  • Photorealistic 3D digital proof rendering
  • Physical pre-production prototype on request
Quote Turnaround: Instant
04 Step 04

Production & Global Delivery

Artisan hand-crafted manufacturing and white-glove logistics. We ship to corporate headquarters or drop-ship directly to individual recipient home addresses globally.

  • Standard lead time 5–7 days; 48-hour rush available
  • Individual global drop-shipping to 120+ countries
  • Real-time tracking via Client Portal Dashboard
Delivery: 5–7 Days / Rush 48h
